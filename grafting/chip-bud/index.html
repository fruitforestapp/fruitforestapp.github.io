Chip bud graft

Best for: Adding one bud to a young rootstock or branch. Very efficient, using one bud per graft.

WhenMid–late summer, or spring
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. Cut a budstick of the current season's growth with plump leaf buds. Clip off the leaves, leaving a short stub of leaf stalk.
  2. On the rootstock, 6–10 in (15–25 cm) above the soil, make a short downward cut at 45° about ¼ in (5 mm) deep. Then, starting about 1 in (2.5 cm) higher, slice down behind it to remove a chip.
  3. Cut an identical chip containing one bud from the budstick.
  4. Fit the bud chip into the notch on the stock, lining up the cambium on at least one side.
  5. Wrap tightly with budding tape. Cover the bud with a single thin layer of Parafilm, or leave it just exposed.
  6. After 3–4 weeks, remove the tape. A green, plump chip means success.
  7. The next late winter, cut the rootstock off just above the bud so all growth goes into it.
