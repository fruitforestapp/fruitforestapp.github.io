Side veneer graft

Best for: Small potted seedlings and evergreens such as citrus, avocado, mango and loquat. The stock keeps its top until the graft takes.

WhenWarm weather while growing, or late winter for deciduous trees
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. Choose a scion about as thick as the stock, with a plump tip bud. Trim evergreen leaves to short stubs.
  2. On a smooth side of the stock, 2–6 in (5–15 cm) above the soil, make a shallow downward cut about 1–1.5 in (3–4 cm) long, just under the bark into a sliver of wood.
  3. At the bottom of that cut, make a short inward cut at an angle to remove the sliver, leaving a small lip.
  4. Cut the base of the scion to match: one long flat face the same length, and a short angled cut on the other side.
  5. Set the scion into the cut with its long face against the stock, lining up the cambium on at least one side, and seat the base in the lip.
  6. Wrap firmly with grafting tape from below to above the union, and cover the whole scion in stretched Parafilm or a small clear bag to keep it moist.
  7. Once the scion is growing strongly, cut the stock off just above the graft, in one or two steps.

Fruits this works well for

🍊 Oranges 🍊 Mandarins 🥑 Avocados 🟠 Loquats 🥭 Mangoes
