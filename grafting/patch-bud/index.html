Patch bud graft

Best for: Thick-barked nut trees such as walnut, pecan and pistachio, where other buds fail.

WhenLate spring to summer, when the bark slips and it is warm
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. Cut a budstick of this season's growth with plump buds. Clip off the leaves, leaving a short stalk.
  2. With a double-bladed knife or two parallel cuts, cut a rectangle of bark about 1 × ¾ in (2.5 × 2 cm) around one bud, and lift the patch off cleanly, keeping the core of the bud attached.
  3. Cut a rectangle of the same size on a smooth part of the stock and remove that bark.
  4. Set the bud patch into the opening at once, so its edges meet the stock's bark.
  5. Wrap tightly with budding tape or plastic strip, leaving only the bud exposed.
  6. After 3–4 weeks, loosen the wrap. A green patch and a plump bud mean it took.
  7. Cut the stock off above the bud in late winter or the following spring.

Fruits this works well for

🥜 Pecans 🌰 Walnuts 🌰 Black walnuts 🟢 Pistachios
