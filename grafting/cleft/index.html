Cleft graft

Best for: Top-working a branch or young trunk 1–4 in (2.5–10 cm) thick to a new variety.

WhenEarly spring, around bud break
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Saw the branch off squarely where the bark is smooth and free of knots.
  2. Split the stub down the middle about 2 in (5 cm) with a cleft tool or heavy knife. Wedge the split open.
  3. Cut the base of each scion (3 buds) into a long, smooth wedge about 1.5 in (4 cm) long. Make the outer edge slightly thicker than the inner edge.
  4. Insert one scion at each edge of the split, tilted slightly outward, so the scion's cambium meets the stock's cambium just inside the bark.
  5. Remove the wedge. The stub will clamp the scions tight.
  6. Wrap the top of the stub and the split down the sides in grafting tape, stretched tight so no cut surface is left open. Wrap each scion completely, tip and buds included, in stretched grafting tape such as Parafilm.
  7. If both scions take, keep the stronger one as the new limb and cut the weaker one back gradually over 1–2 years.
