T-bud (shield bud) graft

Best for: Nursery budding onto vigorous rootstocks. Fast once you have practiced.

WhenMid–late summer, while the bark slips
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. Make a T-shaped cut through the rootstock bark: a 1 in (2.5 cm) vertical cut and a short cross cut at its top.
  2. Slice a shield-shaped piece of bark holding one bud from the budstick, scooping from below the bud to above it.
  3. Gently open the bark flaps and slide the shield down into the T until it is fully seated.
  4. Wrap firmly above and below the bud with a budding strip, leaving the bud itself exposed.
  5. After 2–3 weeks, nudge the leaf stalk. If it falls off cleanly, the bud has taken. If it shrivels and sticks, it failed.
  6. Cut the rootstock off above the bud the following late winter.

Fruits this works well for

🍎 Apples 🍒 Sweet cherries 🍒 Tart cherries 🟣 Plums 🍑 Peaches 🍑 Nectarines 🟠 Apricots 🌰 Almonds 🍊 Oranges 🍊 Mandarins 🍊 Grapefruit 🍋 Lemons 🟢 Limes 🟢 Pistachios
