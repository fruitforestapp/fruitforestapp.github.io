🟠 Limoncella

Loquat · Eriobotrya japonica

Limoncella is a loquat variety from Sicilia, Italy. USDA GRIN accession PI 4567.

First described in 1831. Received by the National Fruit Trials in 1973 from Italy. Fruits have tough flesh with a very sweet flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
