🟠 Golden Nugget

Loquat · Eriobotrya japonica

Golden Nugget is a loquat variety from Florida, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 689604.

Raised in 1932 by C.J. Bishop, Kentville, Nova Scotia, Canada. It was introduced in 1964. Fruits have a sweet, rich flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
