🍊 Owari Satsuma

Mandarin · Citrus reticulata

Owari Satsuma is a Satsuma mandarin variety from Japan. Seedless, very easy to peel; the most cold-hardy (to about 20°F).

FruitMandarins
OriginJapan
TypeSatsuma
Hardiness zones8–11
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
