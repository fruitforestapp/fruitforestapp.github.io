🍊 Encore

Mandarin · Citrus reticulata

Encore is a mandarin variety from California, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 539511.

Raised by Charles Ross at Welford Park, Newbury, Berkshire. First recorded in 1906. It was introduced in 1908 by J. Cheal of Crawley, Sussex. Received an Award of Merit from the Royal Horticultural Society in 1906 and a First Class Certificate in 1908. Fruits have soft, rather coarse-textured, juicy flesh with a subacid flavour. An excellent cooker.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Encore mandarin is a citrus cultivar. This cultivar is a hybrid between two mandarin oranges (King x Willowleaf), obtained by H.B. Frost, in California. It began to be commercialized from 1965.
