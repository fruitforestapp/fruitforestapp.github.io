🍊 Changsha

Mandarin · Citrus reticulata

Changsha is a mandarin variety from China. Very cold hardy; seedy but sweet.

FruitMandarins
OriginChina
TypeMandarin
Hardiness zones8–10
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile

Growing Changsha

Most mandarins are self-fertile; some hybrids set more fruit with a pollinizer. See the mandarins growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft mandarins.
