🍓 Banner

Strawberry · Fragaria × ananassa

Banner is a strawberry variety.

Banner (originally called Sweet Briar) was a variety of strawberry that grew in the northwestern United States in the early 1900s. The variety was originally named "Sweet Briar" by Thomas J. Loftus, who discovered the berry on his ranch just north of Redding, California. The variety was later developed by J.E. "Ed" Reiter and Reiter's brother-in-law, R.F. "Dick" Driscoll, who marketed them under the Banner Berry Farm's Brand (later known as Driscoll's).
