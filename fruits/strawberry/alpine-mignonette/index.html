🍓 Alpine (Mignonette)

Strawberry · Fragaria × ananassa

Alpine (Mignonette) is an Alpine strawberry variety from Europe. Tiny, intensely aromatic woodland-type berries; no runners.

FruitStrawberries
OriginEurope
TypeAlpine
Hardiness zones3–9
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
