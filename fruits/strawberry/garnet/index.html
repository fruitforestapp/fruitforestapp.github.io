🍓 Garnet

Strawberry · Fragaria × ananassa

Garnet is a strawberry variety from New York, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 551486.

Raised by W.Seabrook & Sons Ltd., Boreham, Essex. It was received by the National Fruit Trials in 1936. Fruits have firm, crisp, fine flesh with a slightly subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
