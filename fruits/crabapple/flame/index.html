🍎 Flame

Crabapple · Malus spp.

Flame is a Siberian crab crabapple variety from Minnesota, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 127919.

Raised in 1925 at Boreham, Essex by W. Seabrook & Sons Ltd. Fruits have fine, crisp flesh with a sweet, subacid, slightly aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
