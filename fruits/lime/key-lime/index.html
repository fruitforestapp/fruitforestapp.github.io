🟢 Key Lime

Lime · Citrus × latifolia, C. × aurantiifolia

Key Lime is a Seedy lime variety from Southeast Asia via Florida Keys. Small, very aromatic, tart; for key lime pie.

FruitLimes
OriginSoutheast Asia via Florida Keys
TypeSeedy
Hardiness zones10–11
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationSelf-fertile
