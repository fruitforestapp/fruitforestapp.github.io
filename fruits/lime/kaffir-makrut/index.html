🟢 Kaffir (Makrut)

Lime · Citrus × latifolia, C. × aurantiifolia

Kaffir (Makrut) is a lime variety from Southeast Asia. Grown mainly for its aromatic double leaves used in Thai cooking.

FruitLimes
OriginSoutheast Asia
TypeLeaf lime
Hardiness zones10–11
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forBaking
