🟢 Australian Finger Lime

Lime · Citrus × latifolia, C. × aurantiifolia

Australian Finger Lime is a Microcitrus lime variety from Australia. "Citrus caviar": tiny pearl-like vesicles in long finger-shaped fruit.

FruitLimes
OriginAustralia
TypeMicrocitrus
Hardiness zones9–11
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
