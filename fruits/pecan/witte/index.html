🥜 Witte

Pecan · Carya illinoinensis

Witte is a pecan variety from Iowa, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 518143.

FruitPecans
OriginIowa, United States
Hardiness zones5–9 (typical for pecans)
Good forEating fresh

Growing Witte

Pecans shed pollen and accept it at different times (type I or II); plant one of each type for good crops. See the pecans growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pecans.
