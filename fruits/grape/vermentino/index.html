🍇 Vermentino

Grape · Vitis spp.

Vermentino is a grape variety from Italy.

Vermentino is a light-skinned wine grape variety used primarily in Italian wines. It is widely planted in Sardinia, Liguria and Tuscany, to some extent in Corsica, in Piedmont under the name Favorita, and in increasing amounts in Languedoc-Roussillon. The leaves are dark green and pentagonal.
