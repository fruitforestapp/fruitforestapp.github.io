🍇 Sémillon

Grape · Vitis spp.

Sémillon is a grape variety from France, first recorded in 18th - 19th century. USDA GRIN accession PI 279591.

Sémillon (French pronunciation: [semijɔ̃] ) is a golden-skinned grape used to make dry and sweet white wines, mostly in France and Australia.
