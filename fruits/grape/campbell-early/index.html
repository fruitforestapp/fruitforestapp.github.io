🍇 Campbell Early

Grape · Vitis spp.

Campbell Early is a grape variety from United States, first recorded in 1890. Pedigree: Moore Early × (Belvidere × Muscat Hamburg).

Campbell Early is a hybrid table grape cultivar (Vitis × labruscana). It is also known by several other names, including Boscoso, Island Belle, Campbell's Early, and Early Campbell. Its fruits are prized for their attractive appearance and sweetness.
