🍇 Niagara

Grape · Vitis spp.

Niagara is an American grape variety from New York, US, first recorded in 1868. Green-white Concord relative, sweet and aromatic.

Originated at the New York State Agricultural Experiment Station, Geneva, USA from a selection made in 1950. It was introduced in 1962. Fruits have fine, crisp, tender white flesh with a subacid to sweet, aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
