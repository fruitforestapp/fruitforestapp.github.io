🍇 Vitis rotundifolia

Grape · Vitis spp.

Vitis rotundifolia is a grape variety.

Vitis rotundifolia, or muscadine or swamp grape, is a grapevine species native to the southeastern and south-central United States. The growth range extends from Florida to New Jersey coast, and west to eastern Texas and Oklahoma. It has been extensively cultivated since the 16th century.
