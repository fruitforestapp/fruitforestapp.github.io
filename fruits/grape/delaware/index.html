🍇 Delaware

Grape · Vitis spp.

Delaware is an American grape variety from Ohio, US, first recorded in 1849. Small pink grape, very sweet; dessert and wine.

The Delaware grape is a cultivar derived from the grape species Vitis labrusca or 'Fox grape' which is used for the table and wine production. The skin of the Delaware grape when ripened has a pale red, almost pinkish colour, a tender skin, and juicy sweet flesh. It has small fruit clusters with small berries that do not have the pronounced 'foxiness' of other V. labrusca grapes.
