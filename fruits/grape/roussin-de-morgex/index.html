🍇 Roussin de Morgex

Grape · Vitis spp.

Roussin de Morgex is a grape variety.

Roussin de Morgex is an Italian grape variety, native to the western part of the Aosta Valley in the municipality of Morgex. It is a pink-skinned teinturier grape that produced a light pink juice. Although it may by used as one of the autochthonous varieties allowed in the red DOC wine from the region, Valle d'Aosta Rosso or Vallée d'Aoste Rouge, in 2010 it was not cultivated commercially; according to wine writer Ian D'Agata nobody had made wine from it in 300 years.
