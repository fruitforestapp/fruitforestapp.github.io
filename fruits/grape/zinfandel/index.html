🍇 Zinfandel

Grape · Vitis spp.

Zinfandel is a grape variety from Croatia. USDA GRIN accession DVIT 8197.

Zinfandel is a variety of black-skinned wine grape. The variety is grown in over 10 percent of California vineyards. The precise geographic origin of the cultivar remains uncertain, though peer-reviewed studies have identified Montenegro as the best candidate for its origin and dispersal.
