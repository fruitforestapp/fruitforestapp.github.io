🍇 Müller-Thurgau

Grape · Vitis spp.

Müller-Thurgau is a grape variety from Switzerland, first recorded in 1882. Pedigree: Riesling × Madeleine Royale.

Müller-Thurgau ( M(Y)UUL-ər TOOR-gow, Swiss Standard German: [ˌmʏlər ˈtuːrɡaʊ]) is a white grape variety (sp. Vitis vinifera) which was created by Hermann Müller from the Swiss Canton of Thurgau in 1882 at the Geisenheim Grape Breeding Institute in Germany. It is a crossing of Riesling with Madeleine Royale.
