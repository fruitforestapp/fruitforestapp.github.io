🍇 Amber

Grape · Vitis spp.

Amber is a Hybrid grape variety from Missouri, United States. USDA GRIN accession DVIT 6.

Raised before 1936 at Chelmsford, Essex by W. Seabrook and Sons. Fruits have tender, firm, yellowish-white flesh.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
