🍇 Stambolleshë

Grape · Vitis spp.

Stambolleshë is a grape variety.

Stambolleshë (lit. 'Istanbulite') is a dark-skinned grape variety cultivated on a limited scale in the area surrounding Kolonjë, in southeastern Albania. Historically grown as a table grape for fresh consumption, Stambolleshë was traditionally regarded as an introduced variety, with its name suggesting a foreign origin. The 1985 Albanian Encyclopedic Dictionary lists it alongside Sulltanina and Çaush among cultivars planted for this purpose during the Ottoman period.
