🫐 Borealis

Haskap · Lonicera caerulea

Borealis is a Saskatchewan haskap variety from Saskatchewan, Canada, first recorded in 2007. Large, sweet berries, compact bush.

Growing Borealis

Haskaps need a second, compatible variety that blooms at the same time. See the haskaps growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft haskaps.
