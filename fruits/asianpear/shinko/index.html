🍐 Shinko

Asian pear · Pyrus pyrifolia

Shinko is a Russeted asian pear variety from Japan, first recorded in 1941. Large russet, rich sweet flavor, the most fire-blight-resistant Asian pear.

Raised in 1931 at Aomori Apple Experiment Station, Japan. Fruits have crisp, greenish white flesh with a sweet, subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Growing Shinko

Asian pears need a second Asian (or early European) pear for pollination. See the asian pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft asian pears.
