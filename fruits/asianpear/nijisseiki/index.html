🍐 Nijisseiki

Asian pear · Pyrus pyrifolia

Nijisseiki is a Yellow asian pear variety from Japan, first recorded in 1888. "Twentieth Century": smooth yellow-green skin, crisp and refreshing.

OriginJapan
First recorded1888
TypeYellow

Growing Nijisseiki

Asian pears need a second Asian (or early European) pear for pollination. See the asian pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft asian pears.
