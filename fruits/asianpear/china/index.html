🍐 China

Asian pear · Pyrus pyrifolia

China is an Ussurian asian pear variety from New South Wales, Australia. USDA GRIN accession PI 133540.

FruitAsian pears
OriginNew South Wales, Australia
TypeUssurian
Hardiness zones5–9 (typical for asian pears)
Good forEating fresh

Growing China

Asian pears need a second Asian (or early European) pear for pollination. See the asian pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft asian pears.
