🍐 Shinseiki

Asian pear · Pyrus pyrifolia

Shinseiki is a Yellow asian pear variety from Japan, first recorded in 1945. Round yellow "new century" pear, crisp, mildly sweet. Partly self-fertile.

Growing Shinseiki

Asian pears need a second Asian (or early European) pear for pollination. See the asian pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft asian pears.
