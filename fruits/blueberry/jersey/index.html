🫐 Jersey

Blueberry · Vaccinium spp.

Jersey is a Northern highbush blueberry variety from New Jersey, US, first recorded in 1928. Old reliable, vigorous, good for baking.

FruitBlueberries
OriginNew Jersey, US
First recorded1928
TypeNorthern highbush
Hardiness zones4–7
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forBaking
