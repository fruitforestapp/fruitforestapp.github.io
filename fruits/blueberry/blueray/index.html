🫐 Blueray

Blueberry · Vaccinium spp.

Blueray is a Northern highbush blueberry variety from New Jersey, US, first recorded in 1955. Large, sweet, aromatic; very hardy.

FruitBlueberries
OriginNew Jersey, US
First recorded1955
TypeNorthern highbush
Hardiness zones4–7
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh
