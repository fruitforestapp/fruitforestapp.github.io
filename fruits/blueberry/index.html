🫐 Blueberries

Vaccinium spp.

How to grow blueberries at home: hardiness zones 3–10. Blueberries set bigger, earlier crops with a second variety of the same type nearby.

How to grow blueberries

Sun

Full sun.

Soil

Acid soil is a must: pH 4.5–5.5, rich in organic matter, moist but drained.

Spacing

4–6 ft (1.2–2 m) apart in rows 8–10 ft (2.5–3 m) apart.

Planting

Plant in spring. Mix peat or composted bark into the hole and mulch with pine bark or sawdust.

Care

Keep evenly moist. Feed with acid fertilizer (ammonium sulfate). Net against birds.

Pruning

From year 3, prune in late winter: remove the oldest canes and weak twigs.

Harvest

Summer over several weeks, when berries are fully blue and come off easily.

First crop

2–3 years; full crops at 5–6.

Common problems

Soil pH too high (yellow leaves), birds, mummy berry, spotted wing drosophila.

Through the year

Spring

  • Check soil pH; add sulfur if above 5.5.

Summer

  • Net; water; harvest.

Autumn

  • Mulch with pine bark.

Winter

  • Prune old canes.

Grafting blueberries

Rarely grafted Blueberries are grown from cuttings. Grafting is used only to try a variety on tougher roots, or to add a variety to a bush.

What it joins with

Other blueberries, and in hot climates sparkleberry (Vaccinium arboreum).

When to graft

Late winter to early spring.

Scion wood

Cut pencil-thick one-year-old shoots in mid to late winter, while dormant. Keep them damp and cold in the fridge until you graft.

Best methods

  1. Whip and tongue: On canes of matching size.
  2. Cleft: To add a variety to an established bush.

Propagating blueberries

Blueberries root from cuttings in an acidic mix.

From seed

No.

Ways to make more plants

  1. Softwood cuttings: Root in half peat, half perlite under a dome; 4–8 weeks.
  2. Hardwood cuttings: Take 4–6 in (10–15 cm) cuttings in late winter; stick them in the same peat mix.
  3. Mound (stool) layering: Works well on lowbush and half-high types.

Tip: Use peat or pine-bark mix, never garden lime, and rainwater if your tap water is hard.
