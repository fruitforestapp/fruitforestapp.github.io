🫐 Emerald

Blueberry · Vaccinium spp.

Emerald is a Southern highbush blueberry variety from Florida, US, first recorded in 1999. Large, high yield, low chill.

FruitBlueberries
OriginFlorida, US
First recorded1999
TypeSouthern highbush
Hardiness zones8–10
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh
