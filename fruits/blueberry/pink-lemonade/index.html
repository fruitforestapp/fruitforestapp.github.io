🫐 Pink Lemonade

Blueberry · Vaccinium spp.

Pink Lemonade is a Hybrid blueberry variety from US (USDA), first recorded in 2005. Pink berries, sweet, ornamental.

FruitBlueberries
OriginUS (USDA)
First recorded2005
TypeHybrid
Hardiness zones4–8
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh
