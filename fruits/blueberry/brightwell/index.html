🫐 Brightwell

Blueberry · Vaccinium spp.

Brightwell is a Rabbiteye blueberry variety from Georgia, US, first recorded in 1983. Late blooming (frost escape), productive.

FruitBlueberries
OriginGeorgia, US
First recorded1983
TypeRabbiteye
Hardiness zones6–9
RipensMid (Sept)
