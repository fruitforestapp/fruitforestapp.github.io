🍒 Evans

Tart cherry · Prunus cerasus

Evans is an Amarelle tart cherry variety from Alberta, Canada, first recorded in 1920s. Very hardy prairie cherry, bright red, tart and juicy.

FruitTart cherries
OriginAlberta, Canada
First recorded1920s
TypeAmarelle
Hardiness zones3–7
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationSelf-fertile
