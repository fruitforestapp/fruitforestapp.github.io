🍒 Juliet

Tart cherry · Prunus cerasus

Juliet is a Bush cherry tart cherry variety from Saskatchewan, Canada, first recorded in 2004. Sweetest of the Romance series, good fresh.

Growing Juliet

Tart cherries are self-fertile; one tree is enough. See the tart cherries growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft tart cherries.
