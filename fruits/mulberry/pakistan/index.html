⚫ Pakistan

Mulberry · Morus alba, M. rubra, M. nigra

Pakistan is a White species mulberry variety from Pakistan. Very long (3-4 in) red-black berries, sweet. For mild climates.

FruitMulberries
OriginPakistan
TypeWhite species
Hardiness zones7–10
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
