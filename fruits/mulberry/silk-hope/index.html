⚫ Silk Hope

Mulberry · Morus alba, M. rubra, M. nigra

Silk Hope is a Hybrid mulberry variety from North Carolina, US. Black, sweet and tangy, very productive.

FruitMulberries
OriginNorth Carolina, US
TypeHybrid
Hardiness zones5–9
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
