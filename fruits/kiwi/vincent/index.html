🥝 Vincent

Hardy kiwi · Actinidia arguta, A. kolomikta

Vincent is a Fuzzy hardy kiwi variety from California, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 667956.

Originated in France. Described in 1947. Fruits have firm, crisp, creamy white flesh with a subacid and slightly sweet flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Growing Vincent

Hardy kiwis are male or female (except Issai); plant one male for up to eight females. See the hardy kiwis growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft hardy kiwis.
