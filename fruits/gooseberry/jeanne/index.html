🟢 Jeanne

Gooseberry · Ribes uva-crispa, R. hirtellum

Jeanne is an American hybrid gooseberry variety from New York, US, first recorded in 2000s. Red, mildew resistant, sweet.

FruitGooseberries
OriginNew York, US
First recorded2000s
TypeAmerican hybrid
Hardiness zones3–7
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationSelf-fertile
