🟢 Gooseberry

Gooseberry · Ribes uva-crispa, R. hirtellum

Gooseberry is a European gooseberry variety from India. USDA GRIN accession PI 556443.

Raised in England, probably Kent. It was first recorded in 1831. Fruits have coarse-textured, fairly juicy flesh with a very acid and rather astringent flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
