🟠 Chinese (Mormon)

Apricot · Prunus armeniaca

Chinese (Mormon) is a European apricot variety from US (Utah). Late blooming, frost resistant, sweet kernel.

Growing Chinese (Mormon)

Most apricots are self-fertile; a few (Moongold, Sungold, Harcot in some areas) need a partner. See the apricots growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft apricots.
