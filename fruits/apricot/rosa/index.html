🟠 Rosa

Apricot · Prunus armeniaca

Rosa is an apricot variety from Spain. USDA GRIN accession PI 101627.

Rosa 'Anne Harkness' (aka HARkaramel) is an apricot floribunda rose cultivar developed by Jack Harkness in 1979 and introduced into Great Britain in 1980. The rose makes an outstanding cut flower and is exceptionally long lasting in water.

Growing Rosa

Most apricots are self-fertile; a few (Moongold, Sungold, Harcot in some areas) need a partner. See the apricots growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft apricots.
