🟠 Sungold

Apricot · Prunus armeniaca

Sungold is a Hardy apricot variety from Minnesota, US, first recorded in 1960. Very hardy with blushed gold fruit. Plant with Moongold.

Found in 1960 by Alfred & Anthony Caggiano at Bridgetown, New Jersey, USA. Resembles Golden Delicious but has little or no russet.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Growing Sungold

Most apricots are self-fertile; a few (Moongold, Sungold, Harcot in some areas) need a partner. See the apricots growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft apricots.
