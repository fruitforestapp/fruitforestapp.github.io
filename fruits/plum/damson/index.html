🟣 Damson

Plum · Prunus domestica, P. salicina and hybrids

Damson is a European plum variety from Syria / England, first recorded ancient. Small, tart blue-black plums for jam and preserves.

The damson (), damson plum, or damascene (Prunus domestica subsp. insititia, sometimes Prunus insititia), is an edible drupaceous fruit, a subspecies of the plum tree. Varieties of insititia are found across Europe, but the name damson is derived from and most commonly applied to forms that are native to Great Britain.

Growing Damson

European plums are often self-fertile. Japanese and American hybrid plums usually need a partner of the same group (Toka and native plums pollinate the hybrids). See the plums growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft plums.
