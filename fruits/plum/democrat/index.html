🟣 Democrat

Plum · Prunus domestica, P. salicina and hybrids

Democrat is a Japanese plum variety from Australia. USDA GRIN accession PI 129631.

Discovered in about 1900 growing in the orchard of J.D. Duffy near Hobart, Tasmania, Australia. It is known as Tasma in New Zealand. Fruits have very firm, coarse-textured, juicy, sweet flesh but lacks in flavour. In Australia it is said to have been stored for over twelve months.

Growing Democrat

European plums are often self-fertile. Japanese and American hybrid plums usually need a partner of the same group (Toka and native plums pollinate the hybrids). See the plums growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft plums.
