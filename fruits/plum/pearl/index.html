🟣 Pearl

Plum · Prunus domestica, P. salicina and hybrids

Pearl is a European plum variety from California, United States. USDA GRIN accession DPRU 2068.

Raised in 1933 by W. Seabrook & Sons Ltd., at Boreham, Essex. Introduced in 1938. Fruits have firm, rather coarse-textured, fairly juicy flesh with a sweet, slightly acid and pleasant aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Growing Pearl

European plums are often self-fertile. Japanese and American hybrid plums usually need a partner of the same group (Toka and native plums pollinate the hybrids). See the plums growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft plums.
