🟣 Black Amber

Plum · Prunus domestica, P. salicina and hybrids

Black Amber is a Japanese plum variety. Large, dark crimson to black plum with amber flesh.

The Black Amber is a cultivar of the Japanese plum Prunus salicina known for its distinctive dark color and sweet flavor. It is a choice for fresh consumption and is often used in jams, jellies, and other culinary applications. The Black Amber plum is cultivated in various regions around the world, including North America, Europe, and Australia.

Growing Black Amber

European plums are often self-fertile. Japanese and American hybrid plums usually need a partner of the same group (Toka and native plums pollinate the hybrids). See the plums growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft plums.
