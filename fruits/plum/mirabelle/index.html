🟣 Mirabelle

Plum · Prunus domestica, P. salicina and hybrids

Mirabelle is a European plum variety from France, first recorded old. Tiny golden plum, intensely sweet; for jam, tarts and brandy.

Mirabelle plum (Prunus domestica subsp. syriaca) is a cultivar group of plum trees of the genus Prunus. It is believed that the plum was cultivated from a wild fruit grown in Anatolia.

Growing Mirabelle

European plums are often self-fertile. Japanese and American hybrid plums usually need a partner of the same group (Toka and native plums pollinate the hybrids). See the plums growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft plums.
