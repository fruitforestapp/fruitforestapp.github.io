🍓 Caroline

Raspberry · Rubus idaeus, R. occidentalis

Caroline is a Fall-bearing red raspberry variety from Maryland, US, first recorded in 1999. Large, very flavorful, earlier than Heritage.

Originated in Lord Suffield's garden, Blickling, Norfolk. It was known to already be fruiting in 1822. Fruits have firm, white flesh with a sweet flavour.
