🍓 Nova

Raspberry · Rubus idaeus, R. occidentalis

Nova is a Summer red raspberry variety from Nova Scotia, Canada, first recorded in 1981. Hardy, nearly thornless, disease resistant.

FruitRaspberries
OriginNova Scotia, Canada
First recorded1981
TypeSummer red
Hardiness zones3–7
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
