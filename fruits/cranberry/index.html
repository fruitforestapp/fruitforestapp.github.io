🔴 Cranberries

Vaccinium macrocarpon

How to grow cranberries at home: hardiness zones 2–7. Cranberries are self-fertile.

How to grow cranberries

Sun

Full sun.

Soil

Acid, sandy, moist soil, pH 4.0–5.5.

Spacing

1 ft (30 cm) apart; runners fill in the bed.

Planting

Plant in spring in a bed of peat and sand.

Care

Keep consistently moist; never let it dry out. Mulch with sand every few years.

Pruning

Trim runners in spring.

Harvest

Autumn, when berries are deep red.

First crop

3 years.

Common problems

Weeds and drying out.

Through the year

Spring

  • Trim runners.

Summer

  • Keep moist.

Autumn

  • Harvest.

Winter

  • Cover with mulch in cold areas.

Propagating cranberries

Cranberries spread by runners and root easily from cuttings.

From seed

No.

Ways to make more plants

  1. Softwood cuttings: Stick 4–6 in (10–15 cm) runner or upright cuttings in moist sand and peat in spring or summer.
  2. Simple layering: Pin runners onto the soil; they root along their length.
