🔴 Pilgrim

Cranberry · Vaccinium macrocarpon

Pilgrim is a Large-fruited cranberry variety from New Jersey, US, first recorded in 1961. Very large, late.

FruitCranberries
OriginNew Jersey, US
First recorded1961
TypeLarge-fruited
Hardiness zones2–7
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
