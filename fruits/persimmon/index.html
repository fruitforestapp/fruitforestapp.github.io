🟠 Persimmons

Diospyros virginiana, D. kaki

How to grow persimmons at home: hardiness zones 4–10. Asian persimmons set fruit without pollination. American persimmons are male or female; plant a male nearby unless the variety is self-fruitful.

Growing · Rootstocks · Grafting · Propagating · 505 varieties

How to grow persimmons

Sun

Full sun: at least 6–8 hours of direct sun a day.

Soil

Most well-drained soils, pH 6.0–7.5.

Spacing

15–25 ft (4.5–7.5 m).

Planting

Plant in spring; protect the young trunk. Taproot dislikes moving.

Care

Water in the first years. Avoid heavy feeding, which makes fruit drop.

Pruning

Prune in late winter to a modified central leader; remove weak branches.

Harvest

Autumn into winter. Astringent types must be soft as jelly; non-astringent Asian types can be eaten firm.

First crop

3–5 years.

Common problems

Few pests; fruit drop from stress or overfeeding.

Through the year

Spring

  • Plant a male tree if your American persimmons need one.

Summer

  • Water during drought.

Autumn

  • Harvest; let astringent fruit soften fully.

Winter

  • Prune.

Rootstocks for persimmons

The rootstock sets the size, hardiness and often the disease resistance of a grafted persimmon tree. 3 options:

RootstockSizeZones
American persimmon (Diospyros virginiana) seedlingVigorous4–9
Asian persimmon (Diospyros kaki) seedlingVigorous7–10
Date-plum (Diospyros lotus) seedlingVigorous6–9

Grafting persimmons

Usually grafted Persimmons are grafted onto persimmon seedlings and need warmth to take.

What it joins with

American persimmon (Diospyros virginiana) for hardiness, and D. lotus or D. kaki seedlings in mild climates. Asian varieties are sometimes slow or dwarfed on American stock.

When to graft

Spring, after the stock has leafed out and the bark slips.

Scion wood

Collect dormant scion wood in late winter; graft when the weather is warm.

Best methods

  1. Bark (rind): Very successful on persimmons once the bark slips.
  2. Cleft: In spring when it is warm.
  3. Whip and tongue: On small seedlings.

Tip: Persimmon grafts can sit still for weeks before they grow. Don't give up on them too soon.

Propagating persimmons

American and Asian persimmons are grafted onto American persimmon (Diospyros virginiana) or D. lotus seedlings. Persimmons like warmth to graft.

From seed

No. Seed needs about 60–90 days cold.

Ways to make more plants

  1. Bark (rind): In spring after the stock leafs out and the bark slips.
  2. Cleft: In spring when it is warm.
  3. Seed: For rootstocks: 60–90 days cold.
