🟠 Fuyu

Persimmon · Diospyros virginiana, D. kaki

Fuyu is an Asian non-astringent persimmon variety from Japan. The most popular crisp persimmon: squat, orange, sweet; eat firm.

FruitPersimmons
OriginJapan
TypeAsian non-astringent
Hardiness zones7–10
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh

Growing Fuyu

Asian persimmons set fruit without pollination. American persimmons are male or female; plant a male nearby unless the variety is self-fruitful. See the persimmons growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft persimmons.
