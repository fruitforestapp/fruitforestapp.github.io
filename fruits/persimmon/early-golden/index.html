🟠 Early Golden

Persimmon · Diospyros virginiana, D. kaki

Early Golden is an American persimmon variety from Illinois, US, first recorded in 1880. Classic early American persimmon, sweet.

FruitPersimmons
OriginIllinois, US
First recorded1880
TypeAmerican
Hardiness zones4–9
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh, baking

Growing Early Golden

Asian persimmons set fruit without pollination. American persimmons are male or female; plant a male nearby unless the variety is self-fruitful. See the persimmons growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft persimmons.
