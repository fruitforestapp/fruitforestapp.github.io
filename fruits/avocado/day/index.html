🥑 Day

Avocado · Persea americana

Day is a West Indian hybrid (B) avocado variety from Florida, US. Early, good flavor, cold tolerant for a Florida type.

FruitAvocados
OriginFlorida, US
TypeWest Indian hybrid (B)
Hardiness zones10–11
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh

Growing Day

Avocados flower as type A or B. A lone tree crops, but planting one of each type raises yields. See the avocados growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft avocados.
