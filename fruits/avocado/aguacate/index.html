🥑 Aguacate

Avocado · Persea americana

Aguacate is an avocado variety from Copán, Honduras. USDA GRIN accession PI 306120.

Growing Aguacate

Avocados flower as type A or B. A lone tree crops, but planting one of each type raises yields. See the avocados growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft avocados.
