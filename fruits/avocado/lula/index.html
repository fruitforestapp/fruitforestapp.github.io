🥑 Lula

Avocado · Persea americana

Lula is a Guatemalan hybrid (A) avocado variety from Florida, US, first recorded in 1915. Cold-tolerant Florida avocado, rich and productive.

The 'Lula' avocado (Persea americana 'Lula') is an avocado cultivar that originated in South Florida. 'Lula' became a widely propagated avocado after its introduction due to favorable characteristics, including its eating qualities.

Growing Lula

Avocados flower as type A or B. A lone tree crops, but planting one of each type raises yields. See the avocados growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft avocados.
