🥑 Monroe

Avocado · Persea americana

Monroe is an avocado variety from Florida, United States. USDA GRIN accession MIA 35743.

Raised in 1910 by Richard Wellington at New York State Agricultural Experiment Station, Geneva, New York, USA. Introduced in 1947. Fruits have fine, soft, cream flesh with a sweet subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

The Monroe avocado (Persea americana 'Monroe') is a commercial named cultivar of avocado that originated in south Florida. It became popular for its production habits and relative cold-hardiness and is widely grown in Florida.

Growing Monroe

Avocados flower as type A or B. A lone tree crops, but planting one of each type raises yields. See the avocados growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft avocados.
