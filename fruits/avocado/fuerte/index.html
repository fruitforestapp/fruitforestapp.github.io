🥑 Fuerte

Avocado · Persea americana

Fuerte is a Mexican × Guatemalan (B) avocado variety from Mexico via California, first recorded in 1911. Smooth green skin, creamy, rich; was the California standard before Hass.

The Fuerte avocado is the second most popular commercial variety of avocado in the world, after Hass. Brought from Mexico to the United States in 1911, Fuerte quickly became the dominant commercial avocado variety in the United States. Its commercial dominance lasted into the 1950s, when it was surpassed by Hass.

Growing Fuerte

Avocados flower as type A or B. A lone tree crops, but planting one of each type raises yields. See the avocados growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft avocados.
