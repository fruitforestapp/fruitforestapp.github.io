🍎 Wine

Apple · Malus domestica

Wine is an apple variety from United States, first recorded <1850.

Winesap is an old apple cultivar of undocumented origin, dating at least to American colonial times. Its apples are sweet with a tangy finish. They are used for eating, cooking, and are especially prized for making cider.
