🍎 Golden Russet apple

New York, USA, 1800s · Unknown

High-sugar juice — a classic for sweet and hard cider.

Golden Russet is an old American cultivar of domesticated apple which is marketed as excellent for fresh eating as well as for apple cider production. It is a russet apple and is therefore especially used as a cider apple. It is sometimes known as 'English Golden Russet', and has frequently been confused with 'English Russet'.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Dabinett

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
