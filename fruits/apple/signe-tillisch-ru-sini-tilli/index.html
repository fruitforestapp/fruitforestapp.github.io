🍎 Signe Tillisch(ru. Sini Tilliş)

Apple · Malus domestica

Signe Tillisch(ru. Sini Tilliş) is an apple variety from Denmark, first recorded in 1866.

Raised in about 1866 by Councillor Tillisch and named after his daughter. First described in 1889. Fruits have loose, soft, creamy white flesh with a sweet, subacid, aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
