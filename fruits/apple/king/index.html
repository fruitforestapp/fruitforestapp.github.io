🍎 King

Apple · Malus domestica

King is an apple variety from United States.

The King Byerd (or King Bayard) is a cultivar of domesticated apple, that originated in Cornwall. It is primarily used for cooking. The fruit has green skin, which turns yellow when ripened, developing flecks of red and grey russet.
