🍎 Peasgood's Nonsuch

Apple · Malus domestica

Peasgood's Nonsuch is an apple variety from England, first recorded in 1858.

'Peasgood's Nonsuch' is an apple cultivar used both for eating fresh and for cooking. One of the parents is Alexander. Several other apple cultivars( Iedzenu, Reverend W. Wilks, S.T. Wright, Shoesmith, Victory, Alastair Cannon Whithe, John Waterer) are descended from it.
