🍎 Ariane

Apple · Malus domestica

Ariane is an apple variety from France, first recorded in 2002.

Ariane is a modern cultivar of domesticated apple that was recently developed in France for scab resistance. Ariane is mainly served as a dessert apple because of its mild pleasant flavour. The skin has an attractive combination of yellow and red colours.
