🍎 Akane apple

Japan, 1937 (released 1970) · Jonathan × Worcester Pearmain

Tough and productive. Does well in hotter climates than many early apples.

'Akane', also known as 'Tokyo Rose', 'Tohoku No.3' and 'Prime Red', is a Japanese cultivar of domesticated apple, that according to Orange Pippin is one of the best early season apples. 'Akane' was developed by the Morioka Experimental Station of Japan sometime in 1939,by crossing a Jonathan apple with a Worcester Pearmain, and was introduced to the United States, where it is very popular.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
