🍎 Cameo apple

Washington, USA, 1987 · Chance seedling (Red Delicious × Golden Delicious?)

A chance seedling found in an orchard.

'Cameo' is a cultivar of apple, discovered by chance by the Caudle family at their orchard in Dryden, Washington in 1987. Its parentage is uncertain but is thought to be a cross between Red Delicious and Golden Delicious, since it was found near orchards of those fruits. It also appears similar to the original Delicious cultivar.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
