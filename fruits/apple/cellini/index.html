🍎 Cellini

Apple · Malus domestica

Cellini is an apple variety from England, first recorded <1843.

Raised by Leonard Phillips, a nurseryman at Vauxhall, London. Introduced in about 1828. Fruits have rather soft, juicy, coarse-textured flesh with a slightly acid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

'Cellini' is an apple cultivar that was selected from a seed of a tree referred to as 'Nonsuch', though that name is ambiguous. It was introduced in about 1828 by Leonard Phillips, who operated a nursery at Vauxhall, London. It is a soft-textured cooking apple, juicy and coarse-textured.
