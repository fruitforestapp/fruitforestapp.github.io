🍎 Kingston Black apple

Somerset, England, 1800s · Unknown

One of the few apples that makes a complete single-variety cider.

The Kingston Black, also known as Black Taunton, is a cultivar of apple originating from the United Kingdom and used in making cider. The name of the cultivar comes from the apples' dark red or purplish skin, though despite the name, the fruit does not have a black hue.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Dabinett

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
