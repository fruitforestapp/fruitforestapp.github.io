🍎 Winter Banana

Apple · Malus domestica

Winter Banana is an apple variety from United States, first recorded in 1876.

Raised in 1876 on the farm of David Flory near Adamsboro, Cross County, Indiana, USA. Introduced in 1890. Received an Award of Merit from the Royal Horticultural Society in 1912. Fruits have rather soft, rather coarse-textured,moderately juicy flesh with a sweet and pleasant aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

'Winter Banana' is an apple cultivar with high-quality fruit used for fresh eating. The fruit is large, with smooth yellow skin that bruises more easily than red apples do. The flesh is rather coarse textured, moderately soft, sweet and aromatic.
