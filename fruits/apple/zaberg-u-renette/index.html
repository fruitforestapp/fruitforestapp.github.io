🍎 Zabergäu Renette

Apple · Malus domestica

Zabergäu Renette is an apple variety from Germany.

Raised at Hausen on the Zaber, Wurttemberg, Germany from seed sown in 1885. Fruits have firm, moderately fine-textured, fairly juicy flesh with a rich, nutty flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
