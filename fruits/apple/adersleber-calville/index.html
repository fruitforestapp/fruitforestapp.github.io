🍎 Adersleber Calville

Apple · Malus domestica

Adersleber Calville is an apple variety from Germany, first recorded in 1830–1840.

A German variety raised in about 1870 at Adersleben, Saxony by Lichtard. Fruits have firm, fine, white flesh with a subacid, sweet flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
