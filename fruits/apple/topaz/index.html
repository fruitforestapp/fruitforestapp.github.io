🍎 Topaz apple

Czech Republic, 1984 · Rubin × Vanda

Popular disease-resistant variety in European organic orchards. Carries the Vf scab-resistance gene.

Topaz is a cultivar of dessert apple that was developed in Czech Republic by the Institute of Experimental Botany for scab resistance. According to Orange Pippin it is "one of the best modern disease-resistant varieties, with fairly sharp flavour".

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
