🍎 Lord Derby

Apple · Malus domestica

Lord Derby is an apple variety from England, first recorded in 1862.

Raised by Witham, Stockport, Cheshire. It was first recorded in 1862. Fruits are rather coarse textured, somewhat dry with a subacid flavour. Cooks well.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
