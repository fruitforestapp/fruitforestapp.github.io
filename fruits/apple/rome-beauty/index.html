🍎 Rome Beauty apple

Rome Township, Ohio, USA, 1817 · Unknown seedling

A classic baking apple. Late bloom avoids many spring frosts and it is partly self-fertile.

Originated with H.N. Gillett in Lawrence County, Ohio, USA. Brought to notice in 1848. Fruits have creamy white, rather coarse-textured, juicy flesh with very little flavour. Fruits do not mature well in the UK.

Pollination

Partly self-fertile. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Arkansas Black Dabinett Golden Delicious Golden Russet GoldRush Kingston Black Northern Spy

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
