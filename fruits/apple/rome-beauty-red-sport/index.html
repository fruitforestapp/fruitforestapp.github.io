🍎 Rome Beauty (Red Sport)

Apple · Malus domestica

Rome Beauty (Red Sport) is an apple variety.

Originated with H.N. Gillett in Lawrence County, Ohio, USA. Brought to notice in 1848. Fruits have creamy white, rather coarse-textured, juicy flesh with very little flavour. Fruits do not mature well in the UK.
