🍎 Opal apple

Czech Republic, 1999 · Golden Delicious × Topaz

Club variety. Scab resistant from its Topaz parent.

Raised by W.P. Seabrook, Chelmsford, Essex. Received by the National Fruit Trials in 1936. Fruits have firm, crisp flesh with a sweet subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Opal is the brand name for apple cultivar 'UEB 32642'. The variety originates from a natural crossing of 'Golden Delicious' with 'Topaz'. Developed by the Institute of Experimental Botany in Prague, it is grown by FirstFruits Farms LLC in Washington and marketed by FirstFruits Marketing.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
