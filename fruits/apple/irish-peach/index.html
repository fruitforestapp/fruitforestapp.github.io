🍎 Irish Peach

Apple · Malus domestica

Irish Peach is an apple variety from Ireland, first recorded in 19th century.

Raised in Ireland, possibly in Sligo. It was introduced to England in 1820. Fruits have white, moderately firm, juicy flesh with a good aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
