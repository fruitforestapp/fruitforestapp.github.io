🍎 Golden Delicious apple

West Virginia, USA, 1890s · Chance seedling

One of the best pollinators for other apples. Parent of Gala, Jonagold, Mutsu, Pink Lady and many more.

Golden Delicious is a cultivar of apple from the United States, where it is one of the top varieties. Despite its name, it is not closely related to Red Delicious.

Pollination

Partly self-fertile. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Dabinett

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
