🍎 Burchardt's Reinette

Apple · Malus domestica

Burchardt's Reinette is an apple variety from Ukraine, first recorded <1860.

Raised by Von Hartwiss, Director of the Royal Garden at Nikita, Crimea, Russia. Recorded in 1863. Fruits have crisp, white flesh with a sweet, subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
