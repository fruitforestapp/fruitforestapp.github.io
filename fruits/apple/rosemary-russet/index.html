🍎 Rosemary Russet

Apple · Malus domestica

Rosemary Russet is an apple variety from United Kingdom, first recorded in 1831.

Raised in England. First described in 1831. Fruits have firm, fine-textured, juicy flesh with a rather acid and good flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
