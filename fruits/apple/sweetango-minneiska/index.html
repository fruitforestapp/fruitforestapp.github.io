🍎 SweeTango (Minneiska) apple

Minnesota, USA, 1988 (released 2009) · Honeycrisp × Zestar!

Managed (club) variety; trees are not generally sold to home growers.

SweeTango is the brand name of the cultivated apple variety Minneiska, a hybrid between the Honeycrisp and the Zestar apples belonging to the University of Minnesota. The apple is controlled and regulated for marketing, allowing only exclusive territories for growing. It has a sweet-tart taste that some food writers have described as something between brown sugar and spiced apple cider.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
