🍎 Calville Blanc d'Hiver apple

France, Before 1598 · Unknown

The classic French tarte Tatin apple, with distinctly ribbed fruit. Needs a long, warm season.

Raised in Europe, probably France or Germany. Recorded in 1598. Fruits have a rather soft, fine-textured juicy flesh with a rich, sweet, aromatic flavour. Fruits fail to ripen fully in the UK.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

The Calville Blanc d'hiver (White Winter Calville) is an apple cultivar. It originated in France in the 17th century from a chance seedling. The older apple varieties that carry the name "Calville" was very popular in Germany and France and are notable for their unusual look (the sides are somewhat lumpy).

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
