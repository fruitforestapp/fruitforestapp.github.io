🍎 Sunrise

Apple · Malus domestica

Sunrise is an apple variety from Summerland B.C. Canada, first recorded in 1991.

Thought to have been raised at Welford Park, Berkshire, England. Recorded in 1897. Fruits have soft, tender, whitish flesh with a sweet, subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
