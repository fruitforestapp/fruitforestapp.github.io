🍎 Chestnut Crab apple

Minnesota, USA, 1946 · Malinda seedling

A large crabapple that is genuinely good to eat fresh. Great pollinator.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Anna Antonovka Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Dolgo Crabapple

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
