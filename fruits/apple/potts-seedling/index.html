🍎 Pott's Seedling

Apple · Malus domestica

Pott's Seedling is an apple variety from England, first recorded in 1849.

Raised by Samuel Potts at Ashton-under-Lyme, Cheshire in about 1849. Fruits have greenish white, rather soft, fine-textured flesh with a very acid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
