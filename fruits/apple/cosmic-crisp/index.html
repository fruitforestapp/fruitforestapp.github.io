🍎 Cosmic Crisp apple

Washington State, USA, 1997 (released 2019) · Enterprise × Honeycrisp

Bred for Washington growers. Flesh browns very slowly after cutting. Trees are widely sold to home growers now that the exclusive period has eased in some regions.

Cosmic Crisp is an American apple with the cultivar designation WA 38, a cross between Honeycrisp and Enterprise apples. Breeding began in 1997 at Washington State University, and the apple was commercially released in 2019. The Cosmic Crisp has seen strong and growing sales since its launch.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cox's Orange Pippin Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
