🍎 Ashmead's Kernel apple

Gloucester, England, c. 1700 · Seedling (possibly of Nonpareil)

Light cropper but a connoisseur favorite. Store a few weeks before eating.

Ashmead's Kernel is a triploid cultivar of apple. Traditionally, Ashmead's Kernel was thought to be diploid but a poor pollinator.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
