🍎 Primus

Apple · Malus domestica

Primus is an apple variety from Netherlands.

Raised in 1935 at the Horticultural Laboratory, Wageningen, The Netherlands. Fruits have soft, coarse, creamy white flesh with a very sweet flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
