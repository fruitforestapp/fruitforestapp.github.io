🍎 Martini

Apple · Malus domestica

Martini is an apple variety from Germany, first recorded in 1875.

Found in 1875 near Kollmar in Holstein, Germany. Fruits have firm, crisp, creamy white tinged green flesh with a subacid sweet flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
