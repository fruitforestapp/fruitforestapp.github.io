🍎 Dolgo Crabapple apple

Russia (introduced to USA 1897), 1897 · Malus baccata seedling

Makes beautiful jelly. Long bloom time makes it an excellent pollinator for many apples.

TypeCrabapple
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug) · Late Aug–Sept
Hardiness zones2–8
Stores2–3 weeks
Bloom timeVery early

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Anna Chestnut Crab Dorsett Golden Egremont Russet McIntosh Pink Lady Pristine Wealthy Yellow Transparent

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
