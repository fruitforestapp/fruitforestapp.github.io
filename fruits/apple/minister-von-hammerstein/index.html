🍎 Minister von Hammerstein

Apple · Malus domestica

Minister von Hammerstein is an apple variety from Germany.

Raised in 1882 in Geisenheim, Germany by R Goethe and named after the then Minister of Agriculture. Fruits have firm, creamy white flesh with a sweet, subacid flavour.
