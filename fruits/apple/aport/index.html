🍎 Aport

Apple · Malus domestica

Aport is an apple variety from Kazakhstan, first recorded in 1865.

The Aport (Russian: Апорт; Kazakh: Апорт алмасы) is a cultivar group of the domesticated apple (Malus domestica) most strongly associated with the foothills of the Trans-Ili Alatau around Almaty in southeastern Kazakhstan. The fruit is unusually large for a dessert apple: typical specimens weigh between 200 and 500 grams (7.1 and 17.6 oz), and exceptional ones exceed 800 grams (28 oz). Local growers credit the soil and altitude of the Almaty foothills for its strong honey-like aroma and sweet-tart flavour.
