🍎 Close

Apple · Malus domestica

Close is an apple variety from United States, first recorded in 1928.

Triploid. Raised by C.S. Close at Arlington, Virginia, U.S.A. It was selected in about 1925 and introduced in 1938. Fruits have soft flesh with little juice and a sweet but poor flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
