🍎 Reinette de Champagne

Apple · Malus domestica

Reinette de Champagne is an apple variety from France, first recorded c..

Originated in about 1770 in Champagne, France. Fruits have firm, crisp, rather dry, greenish white flesh with a slightly sweet, slightly subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
