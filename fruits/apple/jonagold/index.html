🍎 Jonagold apple

Geneva, New York, USA, 1953 (released 1968) · Jonathan × Golden Delicious

Triploid: cannot pollinate other trees, so plant two other compatible varieties.

Triploid.Raised in 1943 at the New York State Agricultural Experiment Station, Geneva, New York, USA. It was introduced in 1968. Fruits have creamy white, fine-textured, juicy flesh with a sweet and good rich flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Jonagold is a cultivated variety or cultivar of apple originally from New York, United States, but known internationally. Outside of the United States, it is popular in Belgium and the Netherlands.

Pollination

Triploid (needs 2 partners). Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time. Triploid apples make poor pollen, so plant two other varieties that can pollinate each other as well as this one.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
