🍎 Landsberger Reinette

Apple · Malus domestica

Landsberger Reinette is an apple variety from Poland, first recorded in 1840.

Raised in about 1840 by Councillor Burchardt, Landsberg/Warthe, Brandenburg, Germany. It was awarded a First Class Certificate from the RHS in 1882. Fruits have soft, fine-textured very juicy flesh with a sweet and refreshing flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

'Landsberger Reinette' is a cultivar of domesticated apple that originated in Landsberg an der Warthe. Synonymes: Surprise and Reneta Gorzowska. It is also ambiguously known as 'Surprise' apple.
