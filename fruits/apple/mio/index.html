🍎 Mio

Apple · Malus domestica

Mio is an apple variety from Sweden, first recorded in 1932.

Raised in 1932 at the Horticultural Research Station, Alnarp, Sweden by Emil Johansson. Fruits have soft, white tinged pink flesh with a rather insipid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
