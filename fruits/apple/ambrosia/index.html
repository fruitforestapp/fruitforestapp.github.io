🍎 Ambrosia apple

Similkameen Valley, British Columbia, Canada, 1980s · Chance seedling

Found growing in an orchard in BC. Popular with people who dislike tart apples.

Ambrosia is a cultivated variety or cultivar of apple originating in Canada in the early 1990s. The original tree was first cultivated by the Mennell family of Cawston, British Columbia, who discovered it growing from a chance seedling in their orchard in a row of Jonagold apple trees. It is named after the mythical food of the Greek gods.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Antonovka Arkansas Black Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Chestnut Crab Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
