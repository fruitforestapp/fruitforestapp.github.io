🍎 Arkansas

Apple · Malus domestica

Arkansas is an apple variety from New York, United States.

Triploid. Originated near Rhea Mills, Arkansas, U.S.A. from seed sown in about 1833. Introduced in about 1868. Fruits have firm, cream flesh with a subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

The Arkansas Black is a cultivated variety or cultivar of apple that originated in the late-19th century in Benton County, Arkansas. One of its parents is suspected to be the Winesap. The Arkansas Black is not the same as the cultivar Arkansas or Arkansas Black Twig.
