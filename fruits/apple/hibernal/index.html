🍎 Hibernal

Apple · Malus domestica

Hibernal is an apple variety from Russia, first recorded <1870.

Either Russian or originated in the USA and sent to Russia. It is said to have gone to Russia in 1870 and recorded in the USA in 1880. Fruits have firm, coarse flesh with an acid and astringent flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
