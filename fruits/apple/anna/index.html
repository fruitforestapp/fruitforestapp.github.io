🍎 Anna apple

Israel, 1959 · Red Hadassiya × Golden Delicious

Low-chill apple (about 200–300 chill hours) for hot climates. Pair with Dorsett Golden.

The Anna is a dual-purpose cultivated variety (cultivar) of domesticated apple from Israel that ripens early and does well in warm climates.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Chestnut Crab Dolgo Crabapple Dorsett Golden Egremont Russet McIntosh Pink Lady Pristine Wealthy Yellow Transparent

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
