🍎 Purpurroter Cousinot

Apple · Malus domestica

Purpurroter Cousinot is an apple variety from Possibly Holland, first recorded <1760.

Originated in either The Netherlands or Germany. Described in 1766. Fruits have firm, yellowish white flesh with a sweet, vinous flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
