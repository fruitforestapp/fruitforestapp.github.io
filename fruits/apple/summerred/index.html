🍎 Summerred

Apple · Malus domestica

Summerred is an apple variety from Canada.

Raised by K.O. Lapins at Canada Department of Agriculture, Summerland, British Columbia, Canada. Named and introduced in 1964. Fruits have rather soft, very juicy and refreshing flesh with a mild vinous flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
