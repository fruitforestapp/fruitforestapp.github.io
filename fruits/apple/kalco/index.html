🍎 Kalco

Apple · Malus domestica

Kalco is an apple variety from Netherlands.

Raised at the former Kaiser-Wilhelm Institut, Muncheberg, Germany. It was received by the National Fruit Trials in 1967. Fruits have a sweet, subacid, aromatic flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
