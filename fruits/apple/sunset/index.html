🍎 Sunset

Apple · Malus domestica

Sunset is an apple variety from England, first recorded in 1918, named 1933.

Raised in about 1918 by G.C.Addy at Ightham, Kent. Named in 1933. It received an Award of Merit from the Royal Horticultural Society in 1960. Fruits have firm crisp, fine-textured flesh with a good aromatic Cox-like flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

The Sunset is an apple cultivar derived from the Cox's Orange Pippin cultivar. Both are found in Great Britain. The fruit has red stripes and an orange flush over a gold background.
