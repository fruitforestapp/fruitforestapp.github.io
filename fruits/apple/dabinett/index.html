🍎 Dabinett apple

Somerset, England, 1900s · Chance seedling

Small, manageable tree; adds body and tannin to cider blends.

'Dabinett' is an apple cultivar primarily used in cider production in Somerset.

Pollination

Partly self-fertile. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Arkansas Black Golden Delicious Golden Russet GoldRush Kingston Black Northern Spy Rome Beauty

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
