🍎 Golden Spire

Apple · Malus domestica

Golden Spire is an apple variety from England, first recorded in 1850.

Found in about 1850 in Lancashire by Richard Smith of Worcester who also introduced it. Fruits have coarse, crisp flesh with an acid and astringent flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

The Golden Spire apple was first discovered in Lancashire, England in about 1850. It was later grown in Gloucestershire where it was known as Tom Matthews and grown for cider production, although it had previously been used for both cider and eating. It can also be used for juicing and cooking.
