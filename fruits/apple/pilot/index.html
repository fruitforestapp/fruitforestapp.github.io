🍎 Pilot

Apple · Malus domestica

Pilot is an apple variety from Germany.

Raised at the Institute of Fruit Research, Dresden-Pillnitz, Germany. Introduced in 1988. Fruits have firm, sweet flesh. Resistant to fireblight.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
