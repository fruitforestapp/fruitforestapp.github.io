🍎 Aurora Golden Gala

Apple · Malus domestica

Aurora Golden Gala is an apple variety from Canada, first recorded in 2003.

Aurora Golden Gala, originally named 8S6923, is cultivated variety or cultivar of apple that is a cross between the Splendour and Gala. It was named in 2003 in a Canada-wide "Name the Apple" contest. Aurora Golden Gala is a yellow dessert apple.
