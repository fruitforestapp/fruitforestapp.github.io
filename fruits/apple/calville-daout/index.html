🍎 Calville D'aout

Apple · Malus domestica

Calville D'aout is an apple variety from France.

A chance seedling originating in 1925 from France. Fruits have rather sharp white flesh. Cooks well.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
