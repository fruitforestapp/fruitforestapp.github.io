🍎 Arkansas Black apple

Arkansas, USA, 1870s · Possibly Winesap seedling

Store for a couple of months before eating. Weak pollen — rely on other trees to pollinate it.

The Arkansas Black is a cultivated variety or cultivar of apple that originated in the late-19th century in Benton County, Arkansas. One of its parents is suspected to be the Winesap. The Arkansas Black is not the same as the cultivar Arkansas or Arkansas Black Twig.

Pollination

Needs a partner. Apples need a second apple or crabapple variety that blooms at the same time.

Varieties in this library that bloom at about the same time:

Akane Ambrosia Antonovka Ashmead's Kernel Braeburn Calville Blanc d'Hiver Cameo Cortland Cosmic Crisp Cox's Orange Pippin Dabinett Duchess of Oldenburg

Bloom times overlap differently from year to year and place to place. A nearby crabapple is a good backup pollinator.
