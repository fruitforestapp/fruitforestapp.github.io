🍎 Yorkshire Greening

Apple · Malus domestica

Yorkshire Greening is an apple variety from England, first recorded <1803.

Assumed to have originated in Yorkshire, England. It was first recorded in 1803. Fruits have firm, rather coarse-textured, somewhat dry flesh with a very acid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
