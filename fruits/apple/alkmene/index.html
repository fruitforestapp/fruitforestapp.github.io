🍎 Alkmene

Apple · Malus domestica

Alkmene is an apple variety from Germany, first recorded in 1930.

Raised in 1930 at the former Kaiser Wilhelm Institute, Brandenburg, Germany. Fruits are crisp and juicy with a Cox like flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

'Alkmene' is a German cultivar of domesticated apple, also called 'Early Windsor'. Two natural mutations (sports) of this cultivar that have red-skinned fruit have been selected: one called 'Red Alkmene' was trademarked under the name of Red Windsor; the other is 'Ceeval'. It was developed between 1900 and 1949, by the Kaiser Wilhelm Institute in Müncheberg, Germany, by crossing the two cultivars 'Cox's Orange Pippin' and 'Geheimrat Dr.
