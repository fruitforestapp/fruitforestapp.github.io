🫒 Koroneiki

Olive · Olea europaea

Koroneiki is an Oil olive variety from Greece. Tiny fruit, very high oil content, peppery oil.

The Koroneiki (Greek: Κορωνέικη) is an olive cultivar from Greece primarily used in olive oil production. After the Arbequina and Arbosana, Koroneiki olives are among the most common and suitable for high density growing systems around the world. The most common variety for oil production, Koroneiki olives cover 50-60% of the acreage in Greece.
