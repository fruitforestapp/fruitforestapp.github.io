🫒 Cerignola olive

Olive · Olea europaea

Cerignola olive is an olive variety.

Cerignola, also known as Bella di Cerignola or Cerignola Pendola, is an olive cultivar from Italy. Cerignola olives are large in relation to other olive varieties, and mild in flavor. While they are being cured, they may be left in their natural green color, treated to turn them black, or dyed bright red.
