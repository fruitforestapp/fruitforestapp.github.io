🫒 Tanche

Olive · Olea europaea

Tanche is an olive variety from France. Dual-use black olive with a yield of 22–25%.

The Tanche is a French olive cultivar. It is grown primarily in the Drôme and Vaucluse regions of southern France. It is often referred to as a perle noire, the "Black Pearl of Provence".
