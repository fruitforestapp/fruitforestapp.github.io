🫒 Ayvalık

Olive · Olea europaea

Ayvalık is an olive variety from Turkey. Also known as Edremit, it is a popular olive type which is produced mostly for oil.

FruitOlives
OriginTurkey
Hardiness zones8–11 (typical for olives)
Good forEating fresh
