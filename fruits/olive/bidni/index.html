🫒 Bidni

Olive · Olea europaea

Bidni is an olive variety from Malta. Malta's indigenous, disease resistant, cultivar.

The Bidni, which is also referred to as Bitni, is an olive cultivar from the Mediterranean island of Malta. The fruit is small in size, hearty with a "violet colour", and is renowned for its superior oil which is low in acidity. The latter is generally attributed to the poor quality alkaline soil found on the Maltese Islands.
