🥭 Carabao

Mango · Mangifera indica

Carabao is a mango variety from Philippines. The Ataulfo and Manilita mango cultivars originated from the Philippine Mango variety.

The Carabao mango, also known as the Philippine mango or Manila mango among other names, is a variety of particularly sweet mango from the Philippines. One of the most important varieties cultivated in the Philippines, the carabao mango is known internationally for its sweetness and exotic taste. It is named after the carabao, a native Filipino breed of domesticated water buffalo considered the unofficial national animal.
