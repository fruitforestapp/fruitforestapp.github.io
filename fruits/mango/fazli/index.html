🥭 Fazli

Mango · Mangifera indica

Fazli is a mango variety from Bangladesh, India. The "Fazli" mango is a mango cultivar primarily grown in Bangladesh.

The 'Fazli' mango is a mango cultivar primarily grown in Malda and Murshidabad in West Bengal, India and Rajshahi Division in Bangladesh. It is a late maturing fruit, available after other varieties. Fazlis are commonly used in jams and pickles in the cuisine of the Indian subcontinent.
