🥭 Cogshall

Mango · Mangifera indica

Cogshall is a Florida mango variety from Florida, US, first recorded in 1940s. Natural dwarf, good for small yards and pots.

The 'Cogshall' mango is a named mango cultivar that originated in southwest Florida.
