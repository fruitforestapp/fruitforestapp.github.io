🥭 Katchamitha

Mango · Mangifera indica

Katchamitha is a mango variety from India, Philippines. Katchamitha originated from India, but has become one of the most common cultivars in the Philippines where it is commonly called the "Indian mango".

The 'Katchamitha' mango is a mango cultivar originated in India. It is commonly known as Indian mango, and it has become one of the most common cultivars in the Philippines.
