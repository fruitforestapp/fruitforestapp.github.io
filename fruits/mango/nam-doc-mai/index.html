🥭 Nam Doc Mai

Mango · Mangifera indica

Nam Doc Mai is a Thai mango variety from Thailand. Long, golden, honey-sweet, fiberless.

FruitMangoes
OriginThailand
TypeThai
Hardiness zones10–11
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
