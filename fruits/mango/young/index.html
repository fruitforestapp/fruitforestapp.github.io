🥭 Young

Mango · Mangifera indica

Young is a mango variety from United States. Young mango was renamed the "Tebow" (after the American football player) for marketing purposes.

The 'Young' mango, also known as the 'Tebow', is a mango cultivar that originated in south Florida.
