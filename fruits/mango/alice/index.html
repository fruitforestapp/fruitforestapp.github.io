🥭 Alice

Mango · Mangifera indica

Alice is a mango variety from United States.

Raised in 1943 at Balsgard Fruit Breeding Institute, Sweden. Introduced in 1962. Fruits are crisp and juicy but with little flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

The Alice is a mango cultivar that originated in South Florida.
