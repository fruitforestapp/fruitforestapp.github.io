🥭 Imam Pasand

Mango · Mangifera indica

Imam Pasand is a mango variety.

Hamam (हमाम) Mango, Imam Pasand or Himayat or Himam Pasand is a lesser known and exclusive mango cultivar, grown in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Tamil Nadu states in India. The names suggest regal origins and it is said to have been the fruit of choice for India's royalty. It is available only during the months of May and June and each mango could grow up to 800 grams in weight.
