🥭 Mallika

Mango · Mangifera indica

Mallika is a mango variety from India, Nepal, United States. USDA GRIN accession MIA 36627.

The 'Mallika' mango is the result of the hybridization of the Indian mango varieties Neelum and Dasheri. The variety was developed by Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI) in New Delhi and hybridization by Dr. Ramnath Singh.
