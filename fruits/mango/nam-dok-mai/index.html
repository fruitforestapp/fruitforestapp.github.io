🥭 Nam Dok Mai

Mango · Mangifera indica

Nam Dok Mai is a mango variety from Thailand. USDA GRIN accession MIA 22421.

The Nam Dok Mai mango (Thai: น้ำดอกไม้, also spelled Nam Doc Mai) is a mango cultivar which originated in Thailand. It is the most popular mango variety in Thailand, and is grown commercially in Australia and Colombia as well, albeit as a minor variety. The Nam Dok Mai tree is medium-sized, and can potentially reach heights of over six metres.
