⚫ Natchez

Blackberry · Rubus spp.

Natchez is a Thornless erect blackberry variety from Arkansas, US, first recorded in 2007. Early, very large berries.

FruitBlackberries
OriginArkansas, US
First recorded2007
TypeThornless erect
Hardiness zones6–9
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
