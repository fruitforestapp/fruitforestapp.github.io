🌰 Bouche rouge

Chestnut · Castanea mollissima and hybrids

Bouche rouge is a chestnut variety.

Bouche rouge is a variety of European chestnut native to the Ardèche, specifically around the town of Saint-Etienne-de-Boulogne. This cultivar may be the result of a hybridization of the cultivar Sardonne with a local variety. Bouche rouge produces chestnuts of medium to large size, very beautiful appearance and with very good flavor.
