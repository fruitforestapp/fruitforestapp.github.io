🌰 Marigoule

Chestnut · Castanea mollissima and hybrids

Marigoule is a chestnut variety.

Marigoule (a contraction of Marron of Migoule) is the name of a French hybrid of chestnut (synonym M.15 or CA 15), cross between a European chestnut (Castanea sativa) and Japanese (Castanea crenata). In 1986, it originated from a Migoule orchard in Ussac in Corrèze. It is planted in sunny, low altitude areas protected from the wind (up to 300 m elevation for South-West orchard orientation or up to 400 m elevation in South-East orchard orientation).
