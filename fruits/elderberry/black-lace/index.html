⚫ Black Lace

Elderberry · Sambucus canadensis, S. nigra

Black Lace is a European ornamental elderberry variety from England. Dark lacy foliage, pink flowers; edible berries.

FruitElderberries
OriginEngland
TypeEuropean ornamental
Hardiness zones4–8
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh
