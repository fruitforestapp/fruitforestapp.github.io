⚫ Ranch

Elderberry · Sambucus canadensis, S. nigra

Ranch is an American elderberry variety from Missouri, US. Compact, sturdy, productive.

FruitElderberries
OriginMissouri, US
TypeAmerican
Hardiness zones4–8
