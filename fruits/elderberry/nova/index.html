⚫ Nova

Elderberry · Sambucus canadensis, S. nigra

Nova is an American elderberry variety from Nova Scotia, Canada, first recorded in 1959. Large sweet berries, ripen evenly.

FruitElderberries
OriginNova Scotia, Canada
First recorded1959
TypeAmerican
Hardiness zones3–8
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh
