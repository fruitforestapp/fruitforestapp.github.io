⚫ York

Elderberry · Sambucus canadensis, S. nigra

York is an American elderberry variety from New York, US, first recorded in 1964. Very large berries; pair with Adams.

FruitElderberries
OriginNew York, US
First recorded1964
TypeAmerican
Hardiness zones3–8
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh

Growing York

Elderberries set heavier crops with two varieties. See the elderberries growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft elderberries.
