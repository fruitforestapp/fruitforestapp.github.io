⚫ Adams

Elderberry · Sambucus canadensis, S. nigra

Adams is an American elderberry variety from New York, US, first recorded in 1926. Large clusters, vigorous, productive.

FruitElderberries
OriginNew York, US
First recorded1926
TypeAmerican
Hardiness zones3–8
