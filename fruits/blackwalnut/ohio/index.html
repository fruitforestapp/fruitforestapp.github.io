🌰 Ohio

Black walnut · Juglans nigra

Ohio is a Named black walnut variety from Ohio, US. Productive, good kernel percentage.

FruitBlack walnuts
OriginOhio, US
TypeNamed
Hardiness zones4–9
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
