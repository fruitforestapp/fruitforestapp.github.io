🌰 Sparrow

Black walnut · Juglans nigra

Sparrow is a Named black walnut variety from Illinois, US. Very high kernel percentage.

FruitBlack walnuts
OriginIllinois, US
TypeNamed
Hardiness zones4–9
RipensMid (Sept)
PollinationPartly self-fertile
Good forEating fresh
