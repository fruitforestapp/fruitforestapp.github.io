🍑 Elberta

Peach · Prunus persica

Elberta is a Freestone peach variety from Georgia, US, first recorded in 1870. Large old canning peach, golden yellow, freestone.

FruitPeaches
OriginGeorgia, US
First recorded1870
TypeFreestone
Hardiness zones5–9
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationSelf-fertile
