🍑 Tuscan

Peach · Prunus persica

Tuscan is a peach variety from California, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 26824.

Raised in 1976 at East Malling Research Station, Maidstone, Kent. Introduced in 1989. Fruits are crisp and juicy.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
