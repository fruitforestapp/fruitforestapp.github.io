🍑 Oriole

Peach · Prunus persica

Oriole is a peach variety. USDA GRIN accession PI 689988.

Raised in 1914 in Excelsior, Minnesota, USA by the University of Minnesota Fruit Breeding Farm. Introduced in 1949. Fruits have soft, coarse flesh with a slightly sweet and slightly subacid flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).
