🥭 Prolific

Pawpaw · Asimina triloba

Prolific is a pawpaw variety from Michigan, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 690787.

Growing Prolific

Pawpaws need two genetically different trees (two named varieties or seedlings) to set fruit. See the pawpaws growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pawpaws.
