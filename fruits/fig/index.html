🟤 Figs

Ficus carica

How to grow figs at home: hardiness zones 7–10. Common figs set fruit without pollination.

Growing · Rootstocks · Grafting · Propagating · 516 varieties

How to grow figs

Sun

Full sun; against a warm south wall in cooler areas.

Soil

Well-drained soil, pH 6.0–7.0.

Spacing

10–20 ft (3–6 m); grows well in large pots.

Planting

Plant in spring. In zones 6–7 wrap or bury plants for winter, or grow in pots moved indoors.

Care

Water evenly while fruit swells. Restrict roots to favour fruit over leaves.

Pruning

Prune in late winter; remove crossing and frost-killed wood.

Harvest

Summer (breba crop) and late summer to autumn (main crop), when fruit droops and is soft.

First crop

1–2 years.

Common problems

Winter cold, birds, fig beetles and nematodes.

Through the year

Spring

  • Cut back frost-damaged tips.

Summer

  • Water; harvest breba figs.

Autumn

  • Main harvest; wrap or move pots before frost.

Winter

  • Protect from hard frost.

Rootstocks for figs

The rootstock sets the size, hardiness and often the disease resistance of a grafted fig tree. 3 options:

RootstockSizeZones
Own roots (cutting, layer or sucker)As the fruit
Fig seedling (Ficus carica)VigorousAs the fruit
Another fig variety (top-working)As the fruit

Grafting figs

Rarely grafted Figs root so easily that they are rarely grafted. Grafting is used to change the variety of an established fig.

What it joins with

Other figs (Ficus carica).

When to graft

Spring, once the tree is growing and the weather is warm.

Scion wood

Cut pencil-thick one-year-old shoots in mid to late winter, while dormant. Keep them damp and cold in the fridge until you graft.

Best methods

  1. Cleft: To change the variety of a fig.
  2. Chip bud: In spring or summer.

Propagating figs

Figs are among the easiest fruits to root.

From seed

No. Most figs grown in gardens need no pollination and their seed is usually hollow.

Ways to make more plants

  1. Hardwood cuttings: Take 8–10 in (20–25 cm) cuttings in late winter. They root readily even in a jar of moist potting mix indoors.
  2. Air layering: Roots in 4–8 weeks in summer.
  3. Simple layering: Low branches of bush figs root where they touch the ground.
