🫐 Autumn Brilliance

Serviceberry · Amelanchier alnifolia and spp.

Autumn Brilliance is a Hybrid serviceberry variety from Illinois, US. Ornamental tree form with good fruit and red fall color.

FruitServiceberries
OriginIllinois, US
TypeHybrid
Hardiness zones3–8
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
