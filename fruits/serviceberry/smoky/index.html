🫐 Smoky

Serviceberry · Amelanchier alnifolia and spp.

Smoky is a Saskatoon serviceberry variety from Alberta, Canada, first recorded in 1952. Classic commercial saskatoon, very sweet, heavy cropper.

FruitServiceberries
OriginAlberta, Canada
First recorded1952
TypeSaskatoon
Hardiness zones2–7
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
