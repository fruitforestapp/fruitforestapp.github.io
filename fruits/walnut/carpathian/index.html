🌰 Carpathian

Walnut · Juglans regia

Carpathian is a Hardy English walnut variety from Poland / Ukraine. Hardy strain of English walnut.

FruitWalnuts
OriginPoland / Ukraine
TypeHardy English
Hardiness zones5–8
RipensMid (Sept)

Growing Carpathian

Walnuts are self-fertile but bloom timing often mismatches; a second variety helps. See the walnuts growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft walnuts.
