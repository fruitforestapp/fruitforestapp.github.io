🌰 Laciniata

Walnut · Juglans regia

Laciniata is a Persian/English walnut variety from England, United Kingdom. USDA GRIN accession DJUG 649.

Growing Laciniata

Walnuts are self-fertile but bloom timing often mismatches; a second variety helps. See the walnuts growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft walnuts.
