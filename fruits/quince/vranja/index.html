🟡 Vranja

Quince · Cydonia oblonga

Vranja is a Common quince variety from Serbia. Huge, pear-shaped, very fragrant.

FruitQuinces
OriginSerbia
TypeCommon
Hardiness zones5–9
RipensLate (Oct+)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
