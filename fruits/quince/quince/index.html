🟡 Quince

Quince · Cydonia oblonga

Quince is a quince variety from England, United Kingdom. USDA GRIN accession PI 91482.

The quince (; Cydonia oblonga) is the sole member of the genus Cydonia in the Malinae subtribe (which contains apples, pears, and other fruits) of the Rosaceae family. It is a deciduous tree that bears hard, aromatic bright golden-yellow pome fruit, similar in appearance to a pear. Ripe quince fruits are hard, tart, and astringent.
