🍐 Seckel

Pear · Pyrus communis

Seckel is a European pear variety from Pennsylvania, US, first recorded late 1700s. Small "sugar pear" with spicy, intensely sweet flesh. Very resistant to fire blight.

The Seckel pear (or sugar pear) is a small, very sweet cultivar of pear believed to have originated in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Growing Seckel

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
