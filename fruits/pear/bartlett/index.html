🍐 Bartlett

Pear · Pyrus communis

Bartlett is a European pear variety from England (as Williams), first recorded c. 1770. Classic buttery, aromatic summer pear that turns yellow when ripe. The main canning pear. Prone to fire blight.

Growing Bartlett

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
