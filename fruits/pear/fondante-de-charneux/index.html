🍐 Fondante de Charneux

Pear · Pyrus communis

Fondante de Charneux is a European pear variety from Charneux, Belgium, first recorded c1800. Also called Charneux, Merveille de Charneu, Köstliche von Charneeux, Légipont, Merveille de Charneu, Waterloo,. Susceptible to fireblight.

Growing Fondante de Charneux

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
