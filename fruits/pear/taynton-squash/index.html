🍐 Taynton Squash

Pear · Pyrus communis

Taynton Squash is a European pear variety from England, United Kingdom. USDA GRIN accession PI 541271.

FruitPears
OriginEngland, United Kingdom

Hardiness zones4–9 (typical for pears)

Growing Taynton Squash

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
