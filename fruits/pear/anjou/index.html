🍐 Anjou

Pear · Pyrus communis

Anjou is a European pear variety from Maryland, United States. USDA GRIN accession PI 153116.

Growing Anjou

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
