🍐 Ruby

Pear · Pyrus communis

Ruby is a European pear variety from North Island, New Zealand. USDA GRIN accession PI 57927.

Raised by W. Seabrook & Sons Ltd., Boreham, Essex. Received by the National Fruit Trials in 1936. Fruits have rather soft, creamy white with pink tinge flesh with a sweet flavour.

Description from the National Fruit Collection, Brogdale (Open Government Licence).

Growing Ruby

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
