🍐 Louise Bonne

Pear · Pyrus communis

Louise Bonne is a European pear variety from Normandy, France, first recorded in 1778. Also called Bonne Louise d'Avranches, Louise Bonne d'Avranches, Bonne Louise of Jersey, Gute Louise von Avranches. Scab susceptible.

Growing Louise Bonne

Most European pears need a second pear variety that blooms at the same time; a few (Kieffer, Seckel) set some fruit alone. See the pears growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft pears.
