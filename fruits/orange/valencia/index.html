🍊 Valencia

Orange · Citrus × sinensis

Valencia is a Juice orange variety from Portugal / Spain via California, first recorded in 1870s. The main juice orange, ripens spring to summer.

The Valencia orange, also known as Valencia Late, is a late-ripening sweet orange cultivar. Its name refers to the orange-growing region of Valencia, Spain, although the variety was introduced to California from England in the 19th century. It is well known for its soft sweet taste.
