🍊 Rio Red

Grapefruit · Citrus × paradisi

Rio Red is a Red grapefruit variety from Texas, US, first recorded in 1984. Deep red, sweet, the main Texas grapefruit.

FruitGrapefruit
OriginTexas, US
First recorded1984
TypeRed
Hardiness zones9–11
RipensMid (Sept)
