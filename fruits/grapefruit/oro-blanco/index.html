🍊 Oro Blanco

Grapefruit · Citrus × paradisi

Oro Blanco is a Pummelo hybrid grapefruit variety from California, US, first recorded in 1980. Sweet, low acid, no bitterness, thick rind.

FruitGrapefruit
OriginCalifornia, US
First recorded1980
TypePummelo hybrid
Hardiness zones9–11
RipensEarly (Jul–Aug)
PollinationSelf-fertile
Good forEating fresh
