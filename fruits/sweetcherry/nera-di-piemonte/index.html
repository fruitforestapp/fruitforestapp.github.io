🍒 Nera di Piemonte

Sweet cherry · Prunus avium

Nera di Piemonte is a sweet cherry variety from Italy. USDA GRIN accession PI 136809.

FruitSweet cherries
OriginItaly
Hardiness zones5–8 (typical for sweet cherries)
Good forEating fresh

Growing Nera di Piemonte

Many sweet cherries need a compatible partner; self-fertile ones (Stella, Lapins, Sweetheart, BlackGold, WhiteGold) can stand alone and pollinate others. See the sweet cherries growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft sweet cherries.
