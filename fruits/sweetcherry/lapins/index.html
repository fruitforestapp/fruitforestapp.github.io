🍒 Lapins

Sweet cherry · Prunus avium

Lapins is a Dark red sweet cherry variety from British Columbia, Canada, first recorded in 1983. Self-fertile Bing-type cherry, large, firm and crack resistant. Lower chill.

Lapins (also marketed as Cherokee) is a cultivar of cherry. It is a hybrid of the Van and Stella cultivars. It has been awarded the Royal Horticultural Society's Award of Garden Merit.

Growing Lapins

Many sweet cherries need a compatible partner; self-fertile ones (Stella, Lapins, Sweetheart, BlackGold, WhiteGold) can stand alone and pollinate others. See the sweet cherries growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft sweet cherries.
