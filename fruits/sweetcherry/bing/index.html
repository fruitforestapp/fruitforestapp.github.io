🍒 Bing

Sweet cherry · Prunus avium

Bing is a Dark red sweet cherry variety from Oregon, US, first recorded in 1875. The classic dark mahogany cherry: large, firm, very sweet. Cracks in rain.

Bing is a cultivar of the wild or sweet cherry (Prunus avium) that originated in the Pacific Northwest. The Bing is the most popular sweet cherry in the United States, making up 50% of Washington's nation-leading cherry crop. It is also a major cultivar in British Columbia, Canada.

Growing Bing

Many sweet cherries need a compatible partner; self-fertile ones (Stella, Lapins, Sweetheart, BlackGold, WhiteGold) can stand alone and pollinate others. See the sweet cherries growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft sweet cherries.
