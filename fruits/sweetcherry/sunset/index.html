🍒 Sunset

Sweet cherry · Prunus avium

Sunset is a sweet cherry variety from New York, United States. USDA GRIN accession DPRU 86.

Raised in about 1918 by G.C.Addy at Ightham, Kent. Named in 1933. It received an Award of Merit from the Royal Horticultural Society in 1960. Fruits have firm crisp, fine-textured flesh with a good aromatic Cox-like flavour.

Growing Sunset

Many sweet cherries need a compatible partner; self-fertile ones (Stella, Lapins, Sweetheart, BlackGold, WhiteGold) can stand alone and pollinate others. See the sweet cherries growing guide for planting, pruning and care, the rootstocks and how to graft sweet cherries.
