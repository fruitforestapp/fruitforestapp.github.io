Runners

WhenSummer, as runners appear
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Choose strong runners from healthy, productive plants. Use only the first plantlet on each runner.
  2. Pin the plantlet onto the soil, or into a small pot of compost sunk beside the parent, with a U-shaped wire.
  3. Keep it watered. Roots take hold in 3–4 weeks.
  4. Cut the runner and plant the new plants out by late summer so they settle before winter.

Fruits that grow well this way

🍓 Strawberries
