Suckers and division

WhenEarly spring, or fall
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Find young shoots coming up from the roots around the edge of the plant.
  2. Dig down beside each one and cut it away with a good piece of its own root.
  3. Replant at the same depth right away, water well and trim off about a third of the top if it's tall.
  4. Only take suckers from plants growing on their own roots. On a grafted plant, suckers come from the rootstock, not your variety.

Fruits that grow well this way

🫐 Serviceberries 🍒 Tart cherries 🟣 Plums 🟤 Jujubes 🔴 Pomegranates 🌰 Hazelnuts 🍓 Raspberries ⚫ Blackberries 🍓 Strawberries ⚫ Elderberries ⚫ Aronias
