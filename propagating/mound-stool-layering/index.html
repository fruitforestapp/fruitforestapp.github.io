Mound (stool) layering

WhenCut back in winter; mound up through summer
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. In winter, cut the plant down to a few inches above the ground.
  2. When the new shoots are about 6 in (15 cm) tall, heap moist soil, compost or sawdust around their bases.
  3. Add more as they grow, up to about 8 in (20 cm) deep. Keep the mound moist.
  4. The buried bases of the shoots grow roots during the summer.
  5. In late fall or winter, pull the mound away and cut off each rooted shoot to plant out. The old plant sprouts again for next year.

Fruits that grow well this way

🟡 Quinces 🌰 Hazelnuts 🫐 Blueberries 🔴 Currants 🟢 Gooseberries
