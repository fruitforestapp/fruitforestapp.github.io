Semi-hardwood cuttings

WhenMid to late summer, when this year's shoots start to firm up
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. Take 4–6 in (10–15 cm) cuttings from this year's growth that is firm at the base but still green at the tip.
  2. Remove the lower leaves and the soft tip; keep a few leaves at the top.
  3. Lightly wound the base by scraping a strip of bark, then dip it in rooting hormone.
  4. Stick in a gritty, free-draining mix, cover to keep the air humid, and give gentle bottom heat if you can (70–75°F / 21–24°C).
  5. Keep in bright shade. Rooting is slower than softwood: 6–12 weeks. Pot up once roots show at the drainage holes.

Fruits that grow well this way

🍋 Lemons 🟢 Limes 🫒 Olives
