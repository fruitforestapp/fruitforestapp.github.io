Seed

WhenCollect in fall; give it a winter, then sow in spring
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Take seeds from fully ripe fruit and clean off the flesh.
  2. Most fruit seeds need a cold, moist winter before they sprout. Mix them with damp sand, peat or coir in a labelled bag and keep it in the fridge (34–40°F / 1–4°C) for the time given for your fruit. Or sow outdoors in fall, protected from mice and squirrels.
  3. Sow in spring in deep pots or a nursery bed, about two to three times as deep as the seed is wide.
  4. Keep evenly moist. Protect young seedlings from strong sun and slugs.
  5. Most named varieties don't come true from seed. Seedlings are new, unknown varieties, but they make good rootstocks for grafting.

Fruits that grow well this way

🍎 Crabapples 🫐 Serviceberries 🍑 Peaches 🟠 Apricots 🥭 Pawpaws 🟠 Persimmons 🍊 Oranges 🥑 Avocados 🟠 Loquats 🥭 Mangoes 🥜 Pecans 🌰 Walnuts 🌰 Black walnuts 🌰 Chestnuts ⚫ Aronias
