Tip layering

WhenLate summer, when cane tips start to arch down
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Bend a healthy cane over until its tip touches the ground.
  2. Bury the tip 2–4 in (5–10 cm) deep in loosened soil, or in a pot of compost sunk beside the plant, and pin it down.
  3. Keep it moist. The tip roots and sends up a new shoot within a few weeks.
  4. In fall or the next spring, cut the cane about 6 in (15 cm) above the new plant and move it.

Fruits that grow well this way

🍓 Raspberries ⚫ Blackberries
