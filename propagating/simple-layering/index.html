Simple layering

WhenSpring, or early summer
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Pick a low, flexible branch that can reach the ground.
  2. Where it touches the soil, scrape a little bark off the underside or make a shallow slanting cut, and dust it with rooting hormone.
  3. Bury that part 3–6 in (8–15 cm) deep and pin it down; tie the tip upright to a small stake.
  4. Keep the soil moist all season. By fall or the next spring it usually has roots.
  5. Cut it from the parent, wait a few weeks for it to settle, then dig it up and plant it.

Fruits that grow well this way

🔴 Cornelian cherries 🟤 Figs 🌰 Hazelnuts 🍇 Grapes 🟢 Gooseberries 🔴 Cranberries 🥝 Hardy kiwis
