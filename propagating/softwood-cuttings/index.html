Softwood cuttings

WhenLate spring to early summer, on this year's new growth
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. In the cool of the morning, cut 4–6 in (10–15 cm) tips of new shoots that snap cleanly when bent.
  2. Keep them in a damp bag in the shade until you're ready; they wilt fast.
  3. Strip the lower leaves, leave two or three at the top and cut big leaves in half to cut water loss.
  4. Dip the cut end in rooting hormone and stick it 1–2 in (3–5 cm) deep in moist half peat (or coir), half perlite.
  5. Cover with a clear dome or bag, or use a mister, and keep in bright shade at 68–77°F (20–25°C). Never let them dry out.
  6. Roots usually form in 3–8 weeks. Open the cover a little more each day to harden them off, then pot them up.

Fruits that grow well this way

🫐 Serviceberries 🍒 Tart cherries ⚫ Mulberries 🔴 Cornelian cherries 🫐 Blueberries 🫐 Haskaps ⚫ Elderberries ⚫ Aronias 🔴 Cranberries 🥝 Hardy kiwis
