Root cuttings

WhenLate winter to early spring, while dormant
DifficultyBeginner

Step by step

  1. Dig carefully near the plant and cut a few pencil-thick roots.
  2. Cut them into pieces 2–4 in (5–10 cm) long.
  3. Lay them flat 1–2 in (3–5 cm) deep in pots or a bed of moist compost.
  4. Keep them moist. Shoots appear in spring; grow them on for a season before moving.

Fruits that grow well this way

🍓 Raspberries ⚫ Blackberries
