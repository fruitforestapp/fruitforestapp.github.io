Air layering

WhenSpring to early summer, when growth is active
DifficultyIntermediate

Step by step

  1. Choose a healthy branch about ½–¾ in (1–2 cm) thick.
  2. Remove a ring of bark about 1 in (2–3 cm) wide and scrape off the green layer beneath it.
  3. Brush rooting hormone on the upper cut.
  4. Wrap the cut in a fist-sized ball of damp sphagnum moss, cover it with plastic and tie both ends; wrap foil over it to keep it shaded.
  5. Check every few weeks and add water if the moss dries. Roots show through the moss in 6–12 weeks (longer for some).
  6. Cut the branch below the roots, pot it up and keep it shaded and humid for a few weeks while it adjusts.

Fruits that grow well this way

⚫ Mulberries 🟤 Figs 🔴 Pomegranates 🍊 Oranges 🍊 Mandarins 🍊 Grapefruit 🍋 Lemons 🟢 Limes
