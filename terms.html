Don't list patented, trademarked or "club" varieties that you aren't licensed to propagate or give away: a plant patent or plant breeders' right covers reproducing the plant, not only selling it.
  • Follow the plant rules where you are. Swaps and gifts must stay within one country. You are responsible for following federal, state and provincial rules for moving and distributing plants, including quarantines and any permits or certificates. These rules apply even when nothing is paid.

  • Be careful: meet in public places, and report anyone who asks you for money or payment details.
